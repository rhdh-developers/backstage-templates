MATERIAL DESCRIPTIONS AND CARE
================================================================================

UPHOLSTERY FABRICS

Performance Fabrics
- Description: Stain-resistant, durable synthetic blends
- Care: Spot clean with mild soap and water
- Best For: Homes with children, pets, high traffic
- Durability: 50,000+ rubs (Martindale test)
- Price Impact: 10-20% more than standard fabric

Natural Fibers (Linen, Cotton, Wool)
- Description: Natural, breathable materials
- Care: Dry cleaning recommended, spot clean with care
- Best For: Formal living spaces, aesthetics
- Durability: 15,000-35,000 rubs
- Price Impact: Standard to 15% more

Luxury Fabrics (Velvet, Silk Blends)
- Description: Soft, luxurious feel, elegant appearance
- Care: Professional dry cleaning recommended
- Best For: Low-traffic areas, formal spaces
- Durability: 10,000-25,000 rubs
- Price Impact: 30-50% more than standard

Leather (Full Grain, Top Grain, Corrected Grain)
- Description: Durable, develops patina over time
- Care: Regular conditioning, spot cleaning
- Best For: Contemporary spaces, longevity
- Durability: 50+ years with proper care
- Price Impact: 40-100% premium

Faux Leather
- Description: Synthetic leather alternative, vegan option
- Care: Wipe clean with damp cloth, avoid harsh cleaners
- Best For: Budget-conscious, animal-free preference
- Durability: 20,000-35,000 rubs
- Price Impact: 10-25% more than standard

WOOD TYPES & FINISHES

Solid Wood (Hardwoods)
- Types: Oak, walnut, cherry, maple, teak
- Durability: Excellent, lasts decades
- Care: Dust regularly, use wood cleaner monthly
- Finish: Natural, stained, painted
- Price: Premium pricing due to durability

Engineered Wood / Plywood
- Description: Layers of wood with veneer
- Durability: Good for most applications
- Care: Avoid excess moisture, dust regularly
- Finish: Veneer or laminate coating
- Price: Moderate, more affordable than solid

Particleboard & MDF
- Description: Compressed wood particles, budget option
- Durability: Adequate for light to moderate use
- Care: Avoid moisture, careful handling
- Finish: Laminate or veneer coating
- Price: Budget-friendly

Wood Finishes
- Natural/Oil: Shows wood grain, requires maintenance
- Stain: Color applied, requires sealing
- Paint: Opaque finish, most durable
- Lacquer/Polyurethane: Glossy or matte protective coating
- Care: Follow finish-specific instructions

LEATHER CARE

Leather Conditioning
- Frequency: Every 6-12 months
- Product: Leather conditioner recommended
- Benefits: Maintains softness, prevents cracking
- Process: Apply thin coat, buff with soft cloth

Leather Cleaning
- Spills: Blot immediately with soft cloth
- General cleaning: Mild soap and water solution
- Avoid: Harsh chemicals, excessive water
- Frequency: As needed for spills, monthly for dust

Leather Aging
- Patina Development: Natural darkening over time
- Desirable: Adds character and uniqueness
- Normal: Some color variation is expected
- Avoidable: Sunlight exposure speeds aging

FABRIC CARE BASICS

Stain Removal
1. Blot (don't rub) fresh stains immediately
2. Test cleaner on hidden area first
3. Use mild soap solution or upholstery cleaner
4. Work from outside of stain inward
5. Rinse with damp cloth if needed
6. Air dry completely

Regular Maintenance
- Vacuum weekly: Removes dust and debris
- Rotate cushions: Distributes wear evenly
- Sunlight: Use curtains to prevent fading
- Humidity: Maintain 40-50% humidity levels
- Professional cleaning: Every 1-2 years recommended

Common Stains & Solutions
- Food/Drink: Blot, mild soap and water solution
- Grease: Sprinkle baking soda, let sit, vacuum
- Ink: Rubbing alcohol on cloth (test first)
- Mud: Let dry, vacuum, then treat
- Wine/Juice: Blot, club soda, mild soap solution

GLASS AND METAL CARE

Glass Surfaces
- Cleaning: Microfiber cloth and glass cleaner
- Frequency: Weekly for best appearance
- Avoid: Abrasive cloths, harsh chemicals
- Fingerprints: Easily visible, frequent cleaning recommended

Metal Finishes
- Brushed Metal: Dry cloth, follows grain pattern
- Polished Metal: Cloth and metal polish for shine
- Stainless Steel: Microfiber cloth, stainless steel cleaner
- Frequency: Weekly cleaning recommended
- Avoid: Chlorine, harsh acids

STONE SURFACES (Marble, Granite, Quartz)

Marble (Softer, High Maintenance)
- Sealing: Required annually or bi-annually
- Cleaning: pH-neutral stone cleaner only
- Avoid: Acidic cleaners, wine, vinegar
- Coasters: Highly recommended
- Heat: Use trivets under hot items
- Maintenance: High - not ideal for high-traffic areas

Granite (Harder, Lower Maintenance)
- Sealing: Every 1-3 years
- Cleaning: pH-neutral cleaner or soap and water
- Avoid: Harsh chemicals, excessive moisture
- Durability: Excellent for kitchens and countertops
- Maintenance: Moderate

Quartz (Low Maintenance, Engineered)
- Sealing: Not required (non-porous)
- Cleaning: Mild soap and water, everyday cleaners OK
- Durability: Excellent, scratch-resistant
- Avoid: Excessive heat directly (trivets still recommended)
- Maintenance: Low

BY COLOR CHOICE

Light Colors (White, Cream, Beige)
- Brightness: Reflects light, makes spaces larger
- Stain Visibility: Shows stains easily
- Care: More frequent cleaning
- Best With: Performance fabrics or leather
- Mood: Airy, sophisticated

Dark Colors (Charcoal, Navy, Black)
- Stain Concealment: Hides stains well
- Light Reflection: Absorbs light
- Durability Appearance: Shows dust more
- Versatility: Works with most décor
- Mood: Sophisticated, cozy

Neutral Tones (Gray, Taupe, Tan)
- Versatility: Works with any décor
- Stain Visibility: Medium
- Durability Appearance: Shows less dust
- Longevity: Less likely to feel dated
- Mood: Timeless, balanced

Bold Colors (Blues, Greens, Jewel Tones)
- Impact: Makes strong statement
- Trend Risk: May feel dated
- Light Colors: Jewel tones age better than pastels
- Best With: Neutral surroundings
- Mood: Energetic, personalized

Pattern Fabrics (Geometric, Floral, Abstract)
- Visual Interest: Adds texture and design
- Stain Concealment: Patterns hide stains
- Trend Risk: Patterns date more quickly
- Mixing: Combine with solids
- Mood: Playful, designed

PRODUCT DIMENSIONS AND SPACE PLANNING
================================================================================

MEASURE YOUR SPACE

Before Purchasing:
1. Measure doorways and hallways (width, height)
2. Measure room dimensions (length, width, ceiling height)
3. Account for other furniture already placed
4. Leave traffic flow space (36-48" pathways)
5. Measure heights from floor (eye level seating, clearance above head)

FURNITURE PLACEMENT GUIDELINES

Seating Arrangement
- Between sofa and chair: 14-18" distance
- Between sofa and TV: 8-10 feet (depends on TV size)
- From coffee table to sofa: 12-18"
- Breathing room from walls: 12-18" recommended

Dining Layout
- Space around table: 36" minimum for chairs and passage
- Large table in room: 2/3 size of room maximum
- Lighting above table: 30-36" above table surface

Bedroom Layout
- Between bed foot and wall/furniture: 24-36"
- Nightstand width: Should not exceed bed width
- Clear path to other furniture: 24" minimum

COMMON FURNITURE DIMENSIONS

Sofas
- Standard depth: 32-40"
- Standard height: 30-34" (seat height 16-18")
- Common lengths: 72", 84", 96", 108"
- Sectional depth at lounge: 40-50"

Chairs
- Standard depth: 30-35"
- Typical width: 28-36"
- Arm height: 22-28"
- Seat height: 16-18"

Dining Tables
- Minimum space per person: 24" width x 15" depth
- Oval tables: 36-48" width (most common)
- Round tables: 36", 42", 48", 60" diameter
- Extending tables: Check maximum extended length

Beds
- Twin: 38" W x 75" L
- Full: 54" W x 75" L
- Queen: 60" W x 80" L
- King: 76" W x 80" L
- California King: 72" W x 84" L

Clearance Around Beds
- Minimum: 24" on sides and foot
- Ideal: 36" for comfortable passage
- Walk-around (no walls): 36" on all sides

CARE TIPS FOR LONGEVITY

General Care (All Furniture)
- Avoid direct sunlight (fade prevention)
- Maintain stable temperature (wood stability)
- Control humidity: 40-50% ideal
- Dust regularly (weekly recommended)
- Rotate cushions (even wear)

Upholstered Furniture
- Professional cleaning: Every 1-2 years
- Sunlight: Protectwith curtains/blinds
- Pets/children: Use slipcovers for protection
- Spot clean immediately: Prevents staining
- Air circulation: Prevents odor buildup

Wood Furniture
- Sunlight: Causes fading and damage
- Moisture: Avoid direct water contact
- Dust: Weekly with appropriate cloth
- Conditioning: Oil or polish as per finish type
- Heat sources: Keep away from direct heat

Leather Furniture
- Conditioning: Every 6-12 months
- Cleaning: Monthly for dust, immediately for spills
- Sunlight: Accelerates aging and fading
- Sharp objects: Protective measures recommended
- Professional cleaning: As needed for deep clean

Metal and Glass
- Dust: Weekly with appropriate cloth
- Glass: Use glass cleaner for spotless appearance
- Metal: Prevent fingerprints with quick wipe
- Avoid: Harsh chemicals, abrasive pads
- Polish: Metal periodically for shine maintenance
