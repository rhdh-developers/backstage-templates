BACK-ORDER AND PRE-ORDER ITEMS
================================================================================

BACK-ORDER ITEMS
Definition: Items temporarily out of stock but expected to return
Status: Marked "Back Order - Expected [Date]" on product page
Lead Time: Typically 2-6 weeks
What Happens:
- Order placed and confirmed
- Payment processed immediately
- Items ship within 1-3 days of restocking
- You're notified via email when item is in stock
- No additional shipping charge for restocks

PRE-ORDER ITEMS
Definition: Not yet released or expected in limited quantity
Status: Marked "Pre-Order - Available [Date]"
Lead Time: Varies from weeks to months
What Happens:
- Payment processed at time of order
- Items ship on or close to release date
- You're notified via email of shipping
- Cancellation allowed up to 7 days before release
- Refund if pre-order is cancelled

SPECIAL ORDER / CUSTOM ITEMS
Definition: Made-to-order items in specific colors, sizes, or configurations
Status: Marked "Custom Order"
Lead Time: 3-4 weeks standard, up to 8 weeks for complex orders
What Happens:
- Order confirmed and payment processed
- Manufacturing begins
- You're notified at key milestones (started, completed, shipped)
- Non-returnable unless defective
- May offer limited exchanges for significant defects

INSURANCE AND CLAIM PROCEDURES
================================================================================

INCLUDED INSURANCE
Every shipment includes basic insurance:
- Standard Shipping: $100 coverage
- Expedited Shipping: $500 coverage
- Overnight Shipping: $1,000 coverage
- White Glove Delivery: $2,000 coverage

ADDITIONAL INSURANCE OPTIONS
Available for high-value items
Premium Coverage: $0.99 per $100 of coverage
- Example: $5,000 item = $49.90 for full coverage
Optional at checkout
Available up to $10,000 coverage

WHAT'S COVERED
- Damage during transit (broken pieces, dents, etc.)
- Loss of package (not delivered)
- Theft from porch/delivery location
- Weather damage during delivery
- Carrier negligence

WHAT'S NOT COVERED
- Damage from customer misuse after delivery
- Wear and tear (not related to shipping)
- Fading or discoloration from environmental factors
- Damage from improper assembly
- Items not properly packaged for return shipping

FILING AN INSURANCE CLAIM
Step 1: Report Damage Within 48 Hours
- Contact support@luxandliving.com or 1-800-589-5483
- Include order number
- Provide detailed photos of damage and packaging

Step 2: Documentation
- Photos of damaged area from multiple angles
- Photos of shipping box and packaging
- Photos of protective materials
- Description of damage and impact on functionality

Step 3: Assessment
- We review photos and claim details
- Assessment typically takes 2-3 business days
- You'll be notified of claim status

Step 4: Resolution
- Approved: Replacement ships or full refund issued
- Denied: Explanation provided (dispute available)
- Partial: Partial refund if item is usable

DISPUTE RESOLUTION
- If claim denied, you may provide additional documentation
- Can escalate to management review
- Can request review by carrier's insurance
- Timeline: 5-7 business days for escalation review

COMMON DELIVERY ISSUES AND SOLUTIONS
================================================================================

PACKAGE NOT ARRIVED BY EXPECTED DATE
1. Check tracking status online
2. Allow 1-2 business days past estimated delivery
3. Contact carrier (number provided on tracking)
4. If no update in 24 hours, email support@luxandliving.com

TRACKING SHOWS DELIVERED BUT NOT RECEIVED
1. Check delivery location (may be left at specific spot)
2. Ask neighbors or building management
3. Verify address with tracking information
4. Contact carrier to confirm delivery location
5. File claim with us within 48 hours

PACKAGE LOST IN TRANSIT
1. Contact carrier with tracking number
2. Carrier initiates search (typically 5 business days)
3. If not found, carrier files claim
4. Contact us immediately at support@luxandliving.com
5. Replacement or refund issued within 7-10 business days

DELIVERY ATTEMPT FAILED
1. Check tracking for reason (not home, refused, etc.)
2. Reschedule via carrier tracking link
3. Or contact carrier to arrange new delivery
4. White Glove: Call 1-800-589-5483 to reschedule

PACKAGE DAMAGED IN TRANSIT
1. Report within 48 hours of delivery
2. Take photos of damage and packaging
3. Email support@luxandliving.com with photos
4. Do not discard packaging
5. Replacement or refund within 3-5 business days

INCORRECT ADDRESS USED
1. Contact support immediately if order hasn't shipped
2. If shipped, contact carrier to redirect
3. May incur additional delivery charge
4. Contact support@luxandliving.com ASAP
