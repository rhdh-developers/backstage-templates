1. Contact customer service with issue
2. Provide detailed description and documentation
3. Allow 24-48 hours for response
4. Work toward mutually acceptable resolution
5. Most issues resolved at this stage

Formal Complaint Process (If Informal Fails)
1. Submit written complaint to support@luxandliving.com
2. Include: Order number, issue details, resolution sought
3. Reference: Mention attempted informal resolution
4. Response: Within 3-5 business days
5. Escalation: To management if needed

- BBB: File complaint at bbb.org (A+ rated)

- Mediation: Available for complex disputes
