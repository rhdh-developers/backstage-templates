================================================================================
LUX & LIVING - RETURN AND REFUND POLICY
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
Lux & Living is committed to your satisfaction. If you're not completely happy with your 
purchase, we offer a comprehensive return and exchange program.

================================================================================
STANDARD RETURN POLICY
================================================================================

RETURN WINDOW: 60 Days from Delivery Date

Your items are returnable within 60 days from the date of delivery. Items returned after 
60 days cannot be accepted except under our Exception Policy (see below).

Return Eligibility:
- Item must be in original, unused condition (unless defective)
- Original packaging materials must be included
- All tags, labels, and protective coverings must be attached
- No signs of wear, stains, or damage from customer use

The following items are non-returnable:
- Clearance or "Final Sale" items (marked as such)
- Outdoor furniture (due to weather exposure)
- Custom or made-to-order items (except due to defect)
- Items purchased more than 60 days ago
- Opened or used pillows, mattresses, and bedding (hygiene)
- Fabric or upholstery with visible damage

================================================================================
REFUND POLICY
================================================================================

REFUND AMOUNTS

Standard Refund (items in original condition): 100% of product price
Restocking Fee (for items showing minimal wear): 15% of product price
Defective Items: 100% refund + return shipping covered

Refund Processing:
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving and inspecting returned items
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method
- If applicable, financing charges and interest are deducted
- Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or defect

RETURN SHIPPING

Prepaid Return Label:
- Most items: Free return label provided via email
- Heavy items (over 100 lbs): Customer pays return shipping
- Defective items: Full return shipping coverage included

Shipping Carrier Options:
- UPS (standard tracking)
- FedEx (standard tracking)  
- Regional carriers depending on location

================================================================================
EXCHANGE POLICY
================================================================================

EXCHANGES
Exchanges are available within 60 days of delivery for:
- Wrong item shipped (our error)
- Defective items
- Wrong size/dimensions
- Color discrepancy between sample and delivered item

Exchange Process:
1. Contact customer service with order number and reason
2. Photo documentation may be requested
3. Prepaid return label issued
4. New item ships upon receipt and inspection of return
5. No additional shipping charge if exchange is our error

DOMESTIC EXCHANGES: 3-5 business days after return receipt
INTERNATIONAL EXCHANGES: 7-10 business days (may vary by location)

================================================================================
DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS
================================================================================

DAMAGE CLAIM PROCEDURES

Report Damage Within 48 Hours:
Damage claims must be reported within 48 hours of delivery for coverage.

Documentation Required:
- Original order number
- Photos of damaged area from multiple angles
- Photos of packaging and protective materials
- Description of damage

Damage Claim Options:
1. Full Refund: 100% refund after inspection
2. Replacement: New item shipped at no charge
3. Partial Refund: For minor damage if customer wishes to keep item

Damage Claim Timeline:
- Claim submitted: Within 48 hours of delivery
- Review process: 2-3 business days
- Resolution: Full refund or replacement within 5-7 business days

MANUFACTURER DEFECTS
- Defects in construction, stitching, or materials
- Structural damage from manufacturer (not shipping)
- Defective hardware or mechanisms
- Faulty upholstery or fabric

Defect Claims: No time limit for reporting manufacturing defects that prevent use

================================================================================
SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES AND EXCEPTIONS
================================================================================

EXCEPTION RETURNS (Beyond 60 Days)
Returns after 60 days may be accepted in the following cases:

Defective Items:
- Manufacturing defects that appear after normal use
- Examples: seams splitting, legs cracking, springs failing
- Lifetime return window for structural defects
- Requires photo documentation and description

Recalled Products:
- Full refund and return shipping covered
- Notification sent to customers
- Extended return window for safety-related recalls

Warranty Claims:
- Our furniture includes manufacturer warranties (see Warranty Policy)
- Warranty items returnable within warranty period
- Varies by product type (see details below)

DAMAGE CAUSED BY DELIVERY
If damage occurred during delivery (not customer fault):
- Report within 48 hours
- Photographs required
- Full refund, replacement, or repair at company discretion
- Return shipping fully covered

ILLNESS OR ALLERGY RETURNS
Items returnable if customer develops allergies or sensitivities:
- Medical documentation may be requested
- Available within 30 days
- Must be unused and in original packaging
- Common triggers: latex, certain woods, fabric treatments

BUYER'S REMORSE / CHANGE OF MIND
- Returnable within 30 days
- Item must be unused and in original condition
- 15% restocking fee applies
- Customer pays return shipping

================================================================================
WARRANTY INFORMATION
================================================================================

STANDARD WARRANTIES

Fabric Upholstery: 3-Year Manufacturer Warranty
- Covers seam separation, spring failure, frame defects
- Does not cover normal wear, stains, or fading
- Covers defects in materials and workmanship

Leather Upholstery: 3-Year Manufacturer Warranty
- Covers structural defects and cracking from manufacturing
- Does not cover normal leather aging or finish wear

Wood Furniture: 5-Year Manufacturer Warranty
- Covers structural defects, splitting, and joint failure
- Does not cover finish wear or minor dents
- Does not cover damage from humidity changes

Mattresses: 10-Year Manufacturer Warranty
- Covers sagging over 1.5 inches
- Covers material defects and seam separation
- Does not cover damage from water, stains, or improper care

Metal/Glass Furniture: 3-Year Manufacturer Warranty
- Covers structural defects and welds
- Covers glass defects
- Does not cover scratches or minor imperfections

EXTENDED WARRANTY OPTIONS
Available at purchase for:
- Accidental damage protection (up to $5,000)
- Stain and liquid protection
- Mechanical failure coverage (for reclining mechanisms)
- Extended warranty (up to 7 years total)

WARRANTY CLAIM PROCESS
1. Contact customer service with order number and issue description
2. Provide photos if requested
3. Inspection process (typically 2-3 business days)
4. Covered items repaired or replaced
5. Repair/replacement shipped within 7-10 business days

================================================================================
RETURN PROCESS STEP-BY-STEP
================================================================================

STEP 1: INITIATE RETURN
- Contact customer service: support@luxandliving.com or 1-800-589-5483
- Provide order number and reason for return
- Receive confirmation and return authorization number

STEP 2: PREPARE ITEM FOR RETURN
- Inspect item to ensure it meets return requirements
- Original packaging should be used if possible
- Do not discard any protective materials
- Ensure all parts and documentation are included

STEP 3: OBTAIN RETURN LABEL
- Return label emailed within 24 hours
- Label includes tracking number
- Most standard items have prepaid labels

STEP 4: SHIP RETURN
- Drop off at carrier location or schedule pickup
- Keep tracking number for your records
- Take photo of package as proof
- Allow 3-5 business days for delivery to warehouse

STEP 5: RETURN RECEIVED AND INSPECTED
- You'll receive email confirmation when return arrives
- Inspection process takes 3-5 business days
- You'll be notified of inspection results

STEP 6: REFUND PROCESSED
- Refund processed within 5-7 business days of approval
- Appears in bank account within 5-10 business days
- Refund confirmation sent via email
- For purchases, additional 2-3 days for credit card posting

TRACKING YOUR RETURN
- Email confirmation sent at each stage
- Use tracking number from return label
- Contact customer service with tracking number for status

================================================================================
COMMON QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Can I return an item without the original packaging?
Yes, but the original packaging is required for optimal protection. Items without 
original packaging may be subject to additional inspection.

What if I'm not home for the return shipment?
You can drop off at any carrier location, or request a pickup appointment online.

Can I return to a physical store instead of shipping?
Yes! Items can be returned to any Lux & Living showroom location. Staff will inspect 
and process your return on-site. Refunds typically process within 3-5 business days.

What if my return is refused or rejected?
You'll receive detailed information about why. Contact customer service to discuss options.

Can I change my refund method?
No, refunds are issued to the original payment method only.

Do I have to pay for return shipping on all items?
Most items have prepaid return labels. Heavy items (100+ lbs) may require customer 
to cover return shipping costs.

What if my item was lost in transit during return?
Provide tracking information and we'll investigate. If confirmed lost, we will issue 
a refund or replacement.
