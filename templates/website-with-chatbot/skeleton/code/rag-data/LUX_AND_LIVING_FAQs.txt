LUX & LIVING - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
================================================================================

================================================================================
ORDERING AND PURCHASING
================================================================================

Q: How do I place an order?
A: Visit www.luxandliving.com, browse our catalog, add items to your cart, and proceed 
to checkout. You'll need to create an account or checkout as a guest. We accept major 
credit cards, digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and financing options (Affirm, Klarna).

Q: Do I need to create an account?
A: No, guest checkout is available. However, creating an account allows you to save 
addresses, track orders, and access order history for future reference.

Q: Can I save items to view later?
A: Yes! Use the "Save for Later" feature to add items to your wishlist. You can view 
and manage your wishlist in your account dashboard.

Q: Is there a minimum order amount?
A: No minimum order required. However, orders under $49 have a $7.99 shipping fee.

Q: What if the website is down?
A: Call customer service at 1-800-LUX-LIVE (1-800-589-5483) to place orders by phone. 
Our phone team is available during business hours: Mon-Fri 8 AM-10 PM EST, 
Sat 9 AM-9 PM EST, Sun 10 AM-8 PM EST.

Q: Can I modify my order after placing it?
A: If your order hasn't shipped yet, contact customer service immediately at 
support@luxandliving.com or call 1-800-589-5483. We can usually modify orders within 
2 hours of placement.

Q: Do you offer gift cards?
A: Yes! Digital and physical gift cards are available in amounts from $25 to $500 
at www.luxandliving.com/giftcards. They never expire and can be used online or in showrooms.

Q: How can I apply a promo code?
A: Enter your promo code in the "Promo Code" field at checkout before completing 
payment. Codes are case-sensitive. Not all codes can be combined.

Q: Can I use multiple promo codes on one order?
A: Generally only one promo code per order. Exceptions may apply for specific promotions. 
See promotion terms for details.

Q: Do you offer student or military discounts?
A: Yes! Students receive 10% off with verified student ID (SheerID). Military receives 
12% off year-round. Verify in your account settings to activate.

================================================================================
SHIPPING AND DELIVERY
================================================================================

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard processing is 1-3 business days, then delivery varies by location:
   - Standard Shipping (5-7 business days): FREE on orders $49+
   - Expedited (2-3 business days): $19.99
   - Overnight (1 business day): $49.99
   - White Glove Delivery (2-4 business days): $199-$499 depending on item

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we ship to Canada, Mexico, UK, EU countries, Australia, and select others. 
International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination. 
Contact international@luxandliving.com for specific details.

Q: How much does shipping cost?
A: Standard shipping is FREE on orders over $49. Orders under $49 are $7.99. 
Expedited and Overnight options available (see above). Heavy furniture may have 
additional shipping charges at checkout.

Q: What areas do you ship to?
A: We ship to all 50 US states, US territories, Canada, and internationally. 
Some remote areas may have limited delivery options or additional fees.

Q: Can I change the delivery address after ordering?
A: Contact support immediately at support@luxandliving.com or 1-800-589-5483. 
We can modify the address if the order hasn't shipped (usually within 2 hours of order).

Q: What is White Glove Delivery?
A: Our premium delivery service includes:
   - Professional delivery team
   - Inside delivery (not just curbside)
   - Furniture placement in room of choice
   - Assembly of basic components
   - Protective covering removal
   - Packaging material removal
   Available for furniture orders $500+ and certain items. Fee: $199-$499 depending on order.

Q: Can I get an expedited delivery date?
A: Yes, Expedited and Overnight options available at checkout. Note that some items 
have longer processing times (custom orders, back-order items).

Q: How do I track my order?
A: You'll receive a tracking email with your carrier's tracking number once your order 
ships. Track your package through the carrier website or via your Lux & Living account.

Q: What if my delivery date keeps changing?
A: Tracking updates reflect carrier updates. Contact support if the changes seem 
significant or if you have concerns about the delivery date.

Q: What if I'm not home for delivery?
A: For standard delivery, the carrier will attempt redelivery. For White Glove delivery, 
schedule a delivery appointment. Most carriers allow you to reschedule online.

Q: Do you deliver on weekends or holidays?
A: Some carriers deliver Saturday. Sunday and holiday delivery is not available. 
Orders ship within 1-3 business days of purchase (excludes weekends and holidays).

Q: What if my package is damaged during shipping?
A: Report damage within 48 hours of delivery to support@luxandliving.com with photos. 
We'll arrange a replacement or refund at no cost.

Q: How are large furniture pieces packaged?
A: Large furniture is professionally packed with cardboard, foam, and protective 
wrapping to prevent damage. Some items ship in multiple boxes.

Q: Do you ship fragile items like glass or mirrors?
A: Yes. We use protective packaging and insurance. Report any damage within 48 hours 
of delivery for coverage.

Q: Can I hold a package at the carrier facility?
A: Yes, contact the carrier directly once you have your tracking number. 
UPS has "UPS Access Point" hold options, FedEx has similar services.

================================================================================
PRODUCT INFORMATION
================================================================================

Q: How accurate are product photos?
A: Our photos are taken in natural and studio lighting. Colors may vary slightly 
depending on your screen settings and room lighting. Request a fabric sample for 
upholstered items for the most accurate color representation.

Q: Can I get a sample of fabric or material?
A: Yes! Request a free sample on any product page, or email 
samples@luxandliving.com. Most samples arrive within 3-5 business days.

Q: What are the dimensions of furniture pieces?
A: Detailed dimensions are listed in the "Specifications" section of each product page. 
We include height, width, depth, and weight. Arm height and seat height are also listed.

Q: Do you have measurements in different units?
A: Dimensions are shown in inches and centimeters on all product pages.

Q: How much weight can this furniture support?
A: Weight capacity is listed in product specifications. For sofas/chairs, this typically 
refers to each seat. Contact support for specific weight capacity questions.

Q: What materials are used in this item?
A: Complete material composition is listed in "Materials" section. This includes frame 
material, cushion fill, fabric content, and legs material.

Q: Is this item sustainable/eco-friendly?
A: Look for the "Sustainable" badge on product pages. We note when items are from 
eco-certified manufacturers or made with sustainable materials. For details, 
contact support@luxandliving.com.

Q: Do you carry outdoor furniture?
A: Yes, our outdoor collection is in the "Outdoor" category. These items are weather-treated 
and appropriate for patio use. Note: outdoor furniture is not returnable due to weather exposure.

Q: What's the difference between indoor and outdoor fabric?
A: Outdoor fabrics are treated to resist UV fading, moisture, and mildew. Indoor fabrics 
offer more variety in textures and finishes but aren't weather-resistant.

Q: Can I custom order a size or color?
A: Yes! Many items are available for customization. Select "Customize" on the product page. 
Custom orders take 3-4 weeks to produce and ship. No returns on custom items unless defective.

Q: How long is the lead time for made-to-order items?
A: Made-to-order typically ships within 3-4 weeks of order confirmation. Some items 
may take up to 6-8 weeks depending on complexity. Lead times are noted on product pages.

Q: Are back-ordered items available?
A: Yes, items marked "Back Order" can be reserved. You'll be notified when the item 
is back in stock, and we'll ship within 1-3 business days.

Q: What's the difference between stock and back-order items?
A: Stock items ship within 1-3 business days. Back-order items ship when restocked, 
typically 2-6 weeks. Lead times are clearly marked.

PRICING AND PAYMENTS
================================================================================

Q: Why do prices change?
A: Prices may change based on promotions, inventory levels, and market conditions. 
Active promotions and discounts are always clearly marked.

Q: Price dropped after I ordered - will you honor the lower price?
A: We offer a Price Match Guarantee within 7 days of purchase. Contact customer service 
immediately with proof of the lower price, and we'll credit the difference.

Q: Do you offer payment plans?
A: Yes! We partner with Affirm and Klarna for flexible payment options on orders $200+. 
Options like "Pay in 4" and monthly installments are available at checkout.

Q: Is there interest on payment plans?
A: Affirm and Klarna offer both interest-free and interest-bearing plans. The option 
you select at checkout will show the total cost and terms.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, 
Affirm, Klarna, and wire transfer (orders over $10,000).

Q: Is my payment information secure?
A: Yes. We use industry-standard SSL encryption and PCI-DSS compliance. Your payment 
information is never stored and is transmitted securely.

Q: Can I pay by check or bank transfer?
A: Wire transfer available for orders over $10,000. Check payments by special request 
only. Contact business@luxandliving.com for non-standard payment arrangements.

Q: Do you offer Net 30 or business payment terms?
A: Yes, for commercial accounts. Email business@luxandliving.com to set up 
a business account with Net 30 or Net 60 terms.

Q: What if my payment is declined?
A: Ensure your payment information is current and that you have sufficient funds. 
Try an alternate payment method, or contact your bank. Our support team can help 
troubleshoot at 1-800-589-5483.

Q: Can I get an invoice for my order?
A: Invoices are emailed automatically after purchase. Resend via account dashboard 
under "Order History," or contact support.

Q: How do I update my payment method?
A: Log into your account, go to "Payment Methods," and add or update your card. 
Existing subscriptions and auto-renewals will use the primary payment method.

Q: Why is the website loading slowly?

If the issue persists, contact support at support@luxandliving.com.

A: Our address verification system may not recognize all addresses. Try abbreviating 
or reformatting your address. Contact support for assistance.

Q: Can I access my account from multiple devices?
A: Yes, your account is accessible from any device. Simply log in with your email 
and password.

Q: Is the website accessible on mobile?
A: Yes, the website is fully responsive and mobile-friendly. Download the app for 
enhanced mobile features.

Q: What browsers do you support?
A: We support Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest versions). For best experience, 
keep your browser updated.
